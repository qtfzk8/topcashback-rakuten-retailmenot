## How Cashback Sites Actually Work (The 30-Second Version)

Here's the unglamorous truth: when you click from a cashback portal to a retailer, the portal earns a commission from the retailer for sending you there. The portal then shares some of that commission with you. That's it. The "rebate" is really a commission split.

The interesting part is *how much* they share. Most portals keep a cut. TopCashback's pitch is that it passes 100% of the merchant commission back to the member and makes its money from ads and sponsored listings on the site instead. That's the structural reason its rates tend to run higher than Rakuten's on overlapping stores — there's no middle slice being subtracted before the number reaches you.

This also explains why rates vary wildly between sites for the same store on the same day. The retailer isn't paying everyone differently; the portals are just deciding how much to keep. So when you're hunting the best cashback for online shopping, you're really hunting the portal with the thinnest margin.
